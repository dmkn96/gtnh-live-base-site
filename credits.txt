Every distributed texture, its original source path and SHA-256 are listed in assets/textures/manifest.json.
Original PNG assets are provided directly in assets/textures/gt/ and can be replaced independently of the viewer.
No Minecraft vanilla textures, inventories, custom player text or world NBT are included.
Texture profiles represent machine types; exact paint, covers, addons and running state are not recorded.
Material icons use original GT item sprites, tinted in the browser using the material colors from MaterialsInit.java and MaterialsAlloy.java in the same release.
Their base/overlay PNGs are also listed in the texture manifest; the selected colors and layer profiles are provided in assets/material-icons.json.
